# Monthly Map Update Watch Prompt

## Purpose

Once per month, scan for map-relevant changes that could matter to GIS, automotive navigation, OEM map releases, NDS.live, NDAS, and map conversion planning.

Run this only during the first briefing of each month’s first week.

## Scope

Prioritize:

- Korea
- North America
- Europe
- Southeast Asia
- Japan when relevant to travel or automotive navigation

## Watch Categories

Look for credible updates in:

- New road openings, major road closures, bypasses, tunnels, bridges, and interchanges.
- Administrative boundary changes, city or district reorganizations, postal/address changes.
- Major building, airport, port, railway, transit, logistics, or urban development changes.
- Speed limit, lane, access restriction, toll, road rule, and regulation changes.
- EV charging, mobility hub, and location-service infrastructure changes.
- Temporary restrictions only when they indicate a repeating operational pattern or need a dynamic layer.

## Source Preference

Prefer official sources:

- Government transport agencies
- City or regional government notices
- Road authorities
- Airport, port, railway, or transit operators
- Official company or standards-body announcements

Use news sources only when official sources are unavailable or when they help interpret impact.

## Output

Include 3-6 items only if they are meaningful.

For each item:

- What changed
- Where it changed
- Date or effective period
- Source confidence
- Map data impact
- Suggested handling:
  - Base map update
  - Incremental tile/layer update
  - Dynamic content layer
  - Watchlist only

## Skip Rule

If there are no reliable, meaningful changes, say so.

Do not fill the section with old or weak items.

## User-Specific Angle

Whenever possible, connect findings to:

- Freshness tier design
- Change detection
- Automated QA
- Provenance
- Validation gates
- OEM release constraints
- NDS.live tile/layer update strategy
